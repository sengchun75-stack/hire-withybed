Every boat has a stove

A solid-fuel stove in the saloon and diesel central heating through the cabins, so a March week is no colder than a September one. The first two bags of coal are in the price; more is sold at the wharf at £11 a bag.

The stove is the reason we hire all year round.

When a week costs more

High season is any hire with a night between 4 Jun 2027 and 2 Sep 2027. Everything else is low season, including the weeks either side, when the towpath is quietest and the light is best.

We take bookings for starts from Fri 5 Mar 2027 to Fri 19 Nov 2027. Weeks and weekend breaks start on a Friday; midweek breaks start on a Monday.
