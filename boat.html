The hour before you leave

On every boat we sit at the helm with you before the engine goes in gear: throttle and gear lever, where the stop is, how to take a bend wide, how a lock works from the inside. The hour is not a demonstration; you steer it.

Then we take you to the first lock and work it with you. Mute Swan and Grey Heron get two hours.
